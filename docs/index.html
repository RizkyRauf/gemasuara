TikTok content management for multi-account creators

Gemasuara helps creators and social media teams organize video content, prepare captions, track posting status, and publish drafts to TikTok through the official TikTok API — all in one structured workflow.

Features

Multi-Account

Connect and manage multiple TikTok accounts with simple aliases.

Content Library

Store videos and captions in structured folders per account.

Status Tracking

Track content status from draft to ready to posted or failed.

Easy Upload

Upload videos to TikTok inbox as drafts with a single command.

How It Works

  1. Authenticate Connect your TikTok accounts securely via Login Kit.
  2. Add Content Upload videos and write captions for each account.
  3. Review & Ready Validate content and mark it as ready to post.
  4. Post to TikTok Send drafts to TikTok inbox for final review and publishing.
