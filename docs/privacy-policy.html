Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Welcome to Gemasuara. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Gemasuara accesses the following information through the TikTok Official API, only after you explicitly authorize our application:

  • Your TikTok Open ID
  • Basic profile information (such as display name and profile picture, if permitted)
  • Access and refresh tokens required to perform authorized actions on your behalf

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or private messages.

2. Content You Provide

When using Gemasuara, you may upload video files and write captions. This content is stored locally on your device or server in an organized folder structure. We do not upload your content to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account
  • Upload videos to your TikTok account through the official TikTok API
  • Manage your content and posting status across multiple accounts

4. Data Storage and Security

Your access tokens and content metadata are stored locally in your environment. You are responsible for keeping your device and credentials secure. We recommend not sharing your .env or token files with anyone.

5. Third-Party Services

Gemasuara uses the official TikTok API. Your use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke Gemasuara's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok account settings. You may also delete your local data by removing the application files.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@gemasuara.id.
